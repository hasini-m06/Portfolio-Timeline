2014 — Origin

WALL·E : Where it started

Decode Log

My obsession with robotics started with a clunky, rusted-out robot that I just couldn't stop watching. It turned my childhood into a marathon of robot and sci-fi movies and shows like lost in space, transformers, Bumblebee, Pacific Rim, but as I grew up, that fascination turned into a drive to build. I spent most of my time learning about the latest updates of robotics and science, fueled by the goal of eventually creating something human-centric.
